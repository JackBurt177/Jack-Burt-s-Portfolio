About

Still in sixth form. Already trusted with real brands.

I'm Jack Burt, 17, studying Business, Marketing and Sport at Wilberforce College Sixth Form in Hull. Outside the classroom I've designed and built the brand identity, marketing and websites for three live businesses: Fairburt Removals & House Clearance, Burtyco Logistics, and my own business, JB Tech Help.

Before any of that, I founded and ran a nutrition business called Nutrivance, and worked with TotalCool, spending a week in their marketing department as well as staffing trade show events including the NEC Caravan and Motorhome Show. I'm currently looking for a marketing work experience placement required for my course, with a longer-term goal of a paid marketing role.

Experience

What actually running things has taught me.

Before I could build brands for other businesses, I had to learn what goes into running one myself, and what it looks like inside a team that already does it well.

  • TotalCool A week in the marketing department, plus trade show event days including the NEC Caravan and Motorhome Show, showed me what marketing looks like inside an established team, not just as a one-person operation.
  • Running JB Tech Help Every job is a real customer with a real problem and a price I quoted myself. It's taught me to communicate clearly, turn up on time, and take responsibility when something needs fixing properly.
  • Nutrivance My first business didn't work out, but it taught me more about planning, budgeting and knowing when to change direction than a classroom could have.
