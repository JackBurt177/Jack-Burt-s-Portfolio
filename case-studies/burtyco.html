About the business

Speed and reliability, nationwide.

Burtyco Logistics provides time-sensitive, nationwide transport including same-day, next-day and overnight delivery, priority timed delivery, wait-and-return jobs, document delivery, and subcontractor work for other logistics companies. The brand needed to feel fast and dependable in equal measure, for businesses who need something moved and can't afford it to go wrong.

What I built

Logo, flyers and business cards, built as one system.

The arrow-through-a-"B" mark was designed to signal speed and direction at a glance, then applied consistently across a full flyer set explaining the service offer and a business card for James Burt.

The website

Burtyco Logistics online

The brand carries through to the live site, which sets out the full service offer, coverage and contact details for a business that runs on being easy to book and quick to reach.

Why it works

Design decisions that fit the brief.

  • Motion built into the mark The arrow cutting through the "B" gives the logo a sense of movement before a customer reads a single word.
  • Consistent system Logo, flyers and business cards all share the same blue and navy palette and type, so every touchpoint reads as one brand.
