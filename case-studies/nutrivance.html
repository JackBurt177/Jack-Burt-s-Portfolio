Founder · Product & Packaging Design

Nutrivance

A health and nutrition brand I founded and ran, selling two supplement products through Amazon UK. I designed the packaging, labels and pouches myself and ran the business as Director of Marketing and Sales, not just the marketing around a product someone else made, the product itself.

RoleDirector of Marketing & Sales
SectorHealth & nutrition supplements
Sold viaAmazon UK
RanNov 2024 – Apr 2026
About the business

Two products, designed and sold from scratch.

Nutrivance sold two products: a High Strength Ashwagandha capsule (6000mg KSM-66, with organic black pepper and Vitamin B6 for absorption) and a Mushroom Coffee superblend (Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, Reishi, a full spread of B vitamins, Ashwagandha and Brazilian Arabica coffee). Both were aimed at people looking for natural support for focus, stress and sleep, sold entirely through Amazon UK. The business is no longer trading.

What I built

The packaging, the labels, the listing, all of it.

Unlike the marketing and brand work on the rest of this site, Nutrivance was my own product from the ground up. I chose the bottle and pouch formats, designed both labels, and built the product photography and listing graphics used to sell them.

Marketing

Trade leaflets for both products.

Alongside the Amazon listings, I put together a leaflet for each product covering the ingredients, the benefits and the offer, used for wholesale and trade conversations outside of Amazon itself.

Why it ended

An honest read on a crowded market.

  • Amazon got too competitive By the time Nutrivance launched, a handful of large players already held most of the market share in both the ashwagandha and mushroom coffee categories, leaving little room for a small brand to compete on price or visibility.
  • What it taught me Running Nutrivance start to finish, product design, sourcing, listing and sales, taught me more about planning, budgeting and reading a market than any of my other work has, including knowing when to change direction.
