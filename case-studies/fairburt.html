About the business

Trust matters more than a sales pitch.

Fairburt offers house removals, probate clearances, commercial clearances and full or partial property clearances across Hull and East Yorkshire. Their customers are often families relocating, people managing a bereaved relative's estate, or solicitors and estate agents, so the brand had to lead with care and reliability rather than aggressive sales language.

What I built

A logo and identity built from the ground up.

I designed the Fairburt "FB" van mark from scratch, an identity that had to work small on a business card and large on the side of a van, then carried it across business cards and print materials in the brand's navy and red palette.

The website

fairburt.co.uk

The logo and brand carry through to the live Fairburt website, the main shop window for the business, built to reassure a visitor who might be dealing with a difficult move or a bereavement, with clear routes to a quote.

Why it works

Design decisions that fit the brief.

  • Navy and red, not loud A restrained palette that reads as professional rather than promotional, appropriate for a brand that often meets customers at a hard time.
  • One mark, many sizes The "FB" van logo was designed to hold up from a business card to vehicle livery without losing detail.
