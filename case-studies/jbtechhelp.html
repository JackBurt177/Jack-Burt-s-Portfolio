About the business

Tech support that speaks plain English.

JB Tech Help offers in-home and phone tech support across Hull, covering computers, Wi-Fi, printers, phones and tablets. The service is built around patience and clear explanations for non-technical customers who just want things fixed properly, with transparent pricing and no hidden costs.

What I built

Brand identity, marketing and the website.

Navy and orange brand identity, a full set of flyers and social graphics explaining the service in plain terms, and the website itself, all designed to make a one-person, home-visit business feel established and trustworthy.
